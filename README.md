# Now Here / Nowhere

## by Brighid Lowe

A piece focusing upon the disembodied nature of words in the limbo of hyper-space. Paradoxically, a sceptical and yet eloquent critique of the often empty and frustrating nature of 'surfing'.

**Technical Director:** Peter Moore _- e-2_

**Produced by:** e-2

**Viewing requirements:** A modern browser

## The port

The piece was two Flash 1 movies: `nowhere.swf`, the title on blue (550 × 280, 12 fps), which then loaded `begin.swf`, words moving across the full window on black (200 × 40 scaled to fit, 10 fps, looping). `index.html` plays both with CSS animations, without Flash:

- The words are drawn from the movies' own embedded Times New Roman outlines, as SVG.
- Each word or line moves and fades through exactly the positions and colours of the original frames, at the original times, smoothly in between.

`index.html` is generated by `_scripts/build.py` from the two SWFs, using [JPEXS FFDec](https://github.com/jindrapetrik/jpexs-decompiler):

```sh
python3 _scripts/build.py --ffdec /path/to/ffdec-cli.jar
```

`video/now-here-nowhere.mp4` is a recording of the original as Flash played it, at its own frame rates with its stepped motion (1920 × 1080; part two plays once). It's made by `_scripts/video.sh`, which needs FFDec and ffmpeg:

```sh
sh _scripts/video.sh /path/to/ffdec-cli.jar
```

## Layout

The original files are at the top of this repository. The current version of the site is in `docs/`, which is what GitHub Pages publishes. The build scripts are in `_scripts/` and work on the files in `docs/`. Summaries and transcripts of the Claude Code sessions are in `_sessions/`.
